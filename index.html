Why Residents Love It

More than just a restaurant

Six reasons this outlet has become the heart of our community

JAKIM Halal Certified

The entire menu — from Muiz Hot Chicken to Nasi Kandar curries — is prepared to strict halal standards, with KKM MeSTI food-safety certification.

Signature Muiz Hot Chicken

Marinated for 12 hours in a blend of 12 local herbs and spices, then double-coated for a crunch that survives the walk home.

Authentic Nasi Kandar

Northern-style Nasi Kandar with kuah banjir — fish curry, beef gravy and sweet soy sauce mixed over steamed rice.

Fast & Friendly Service

Quick turnaround for busy mornings and lunch breaks. Friendly owners, generous portions, honest prices.

Ample Free Parking

No circling, no stress. Unlike the busy Taipan area, Goodyear Court 6 offers convenient hassle-free parking for residents and guests.

Catering & Gatherings

Bulk orders for community events, office lunches and family celebrations — perfect for residents hosting guests.
